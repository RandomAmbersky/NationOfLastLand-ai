Controls

  1. Click "Initialize Game" to start
  2. Click "Spawn Vehicle" to create units
  3. Selection: Click unit (yellow ring) or drag rectangle for group
  4. Ctrl+Click: Add/remove individual units from selection
  5. Move Group: With selected units, click map position
  6. Attack: Click enemy units to attack as group
  7. Alerts: Click yellow (?) markers to investigate
  8. Combat: Watch red attack lines and damage effects
